I am an undergraduate researcher at Hubei University’s Manchester Metropolitan Joint Institute and an incoming M.Eng. student in Communication Engineering at East China Normal University’s School of Excellent Engineers, starting in September 2027. I am currently a research intern in Prof. Lin Yang’s group at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and an Algorithm Research Intern at Kingsoft AI Product Center.

My work explores reliable learning and decision-making, spanning foundation models and reinforcement-learning agents, multimodal perception, and safety-critical control. My open-source project DanKS focuses on reinforcement learning for GuanDan. Previously, I interned at Z.ai and China Telecom Hubei. I am also a Kaggle Expert, ranked in the top 1%.
