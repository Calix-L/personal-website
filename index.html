I am an undergraduate researcher in Electronic Information Engineering at Hubei University and Manchester Metropolitan Joint Institute, graduating in 2027.

I am currently an Algorithm Research Intern at Kingsoft AI Product Center, where I work on foundation-model pretraining and decision-making agents for imperfect-information games. Previously, I worked at Z.ai on document question answering and at China Telecom Hubei on multimodal public-safety systems.

My research interests include foundation models, reinforcement learning, multimodal perception, and safety-critical control. I enjoy building systems where learning, structure, and reliable decision-making meet.
